# TWESE INC — Recovered Site Design Direction

## Three Design Approaches Considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Clinical Field Notes | A document-led health institution expressed through measured typography, strict hierarchy, and modest visual evidence. It signals accountability without heroic storytelling. | 0.04 |
| Research Ledger | An academic working-paper aesthetic built around annotation, chronologies, and structured research surfaces. It would foreground the relationship between operational care and engineering inquiry. | 0.07 |
| Civic Health Architecture | A calm, high-contrast institutional direction that treats site content as a durable public record, using deliberate structural bands and quiet material contrast. | 0.02 |

## Selected Approach: Clinical Field Notes

**Design Movement.** Editorial institutional modernism, informed by clinical documentation and university research communications. The site should feel like a precise public record of one operating clinic and one emerging research initiative, rather than a campaign or technology launch.

**Core Principles.** The layout privileges clear evidence over visual spectacle; recognizability over ornament; hierarchy over density; and care over persuasion. Every component should help a visitor understand the concrete relationship between TWESE INC, Twese Clinic, and Twese Labs.

**Color Philosophy.** The palette is drawn directly from the supplied logos: deep institutional blue `#075095`, Twese blue `#004AAB`, and Twese orange `#F5660C`. White carries most reading surfaces, while a cool pale blue-gray supports quiet separation. Orange is limited to hairlines, signal marks, and compact status treatment so it never competes with the blue institutional structure.

**Layout Paradigm.** The pages use a “field record” spine: numbered or labeled bands, a persistent institutional header, generous left-aligned reading columns, and offset side annotations. Dense information is housed in ordered modules rather than traditional card grids. On smaller screens, the spine collapses into readable vertical sections without losing the sense of sequence.

**Signature Elements.** A slim orange registration line appears at important section boundaries. A restrained blue “signal trace” references the clinic logo’s heartbeat and the Labs mark’s instrumentation without redrawing either official logo. Status items use compact outlined labels rather than progress graphics.

**Interaction Philosophy.** Interactions are calm and purposeful. Links underline or shift to Twese blue, buttons compress slightly on press, and the mobile navigation opens directly and accessibly. There are no counters, scroll theatrics, floating decoration, or attention-seeking hover effects.

**Animation.** Motion is limited to 140–220 ms opacity and transform transitions with a crisp ease-out. Content does not stage-manage the visitor with entrance animations. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.

**Typography System.** Source Serif 4 gives long-form headings and quotations an academic editorial character; Manrope handles navigation, labels, figures, and body copy with clarity at mobile sizes. Headlines are left aligned and measured; labels use uppercase tracked text; body copy is short, factual, and comfortably spaced.

**Brand Essence.** TWESE INC is a community-grounded health organization advancing rural primary care and practical medical technology research in Kabezi, Burundi. **Measured, rigorous, grounded.**

**Brand Voice.** Headlines state the work plainly; CTAs invite collaboration without urgency; microcopy clarifies status and scope. Example: “Primary care built around rural communities.” Example: “Research designed for infrastructure constraints.” Generic welcome language, inflated claims, and fundraising pressure are excluded.

**Wordmark & Logo.** “TWESE INC” is rendered as a clean typographic umbrella identity, distinct from but secondary to the official unaltered Twese Clinic and Twese Labs logo images. Official division logos remain the only division marks.

**Signature Brand Color.** **Twese Blue — `#004AAB`**, sampled from the official Twese Clinic logo and used for active navigation, key actions, and practical information cues.

## Style Decisions

- Official logo files are used unchanged, at readable dimensions, with generous clear space and no recoloring.
- No photos will be substituted or fabricated. Until real Twese action photography is supplied, photo locations remain neutral and explicitly labeled.
- Public statements, statistics, partnerships, milestones, and biographies will only use information provided in the recovery specification; uncertainty is labeled `[VERIFY BEFORE PUBLISHING]`.
- Orange `#F5660C` is reserved for registration lines, section numerals, compact status or verification marks, and signal accents; primary actions and active navigation use Twese Blue `#004AAB`.
- Every major section should read as a field record with a visible label, sequence marker or ledger structure, and an evidence-aware annotation where applicable.
- The `TWESE INC` umbrella identity remains a quiet typographic institutional stamp, consistently paired with its orange registration square and never treated as a replacement logo for either official division.
- The shared header repeats the TWESE INC umbrella stamp beside the unchanged Twese Clinic logo, clarifying the institutional relationship without competing with either official division mark.
- Original-site screenshots establish the ground-truth visual spec: warm ivory section backgrounds, near-black copy, deep midnight navy hero fields, a compact editorial serif used at controlled sizes, and muted gray sans-serif text. The header uses the Twese Clinic logo as its origin mark, with a compact editorial navigation and a navy Partner With Us button.
- Use bordered white record rows for collaboration and directory content. Preserve their spacious desktop proportions, use thin warm-gray rules, and allow mobile to collapse to clear sequential rows.
- **Held reference benchmark:** The supplied original screenshots supersede any looser interpretation of “Clinical Field Notes.” The visual target is almost-black navy (not saturated blue), untextured white content fields, quiet gray body copy, a compact high-contrast serif with the restrained editorial proportions seen in the source, and a clean narrow sans-serif for navigation and metadata. Orange appears only as a fine rule, active-line, or small accent—never as a dominant field or generalized campaign color.
